# Account Deletion

This page explains how to delete your Reel Rivals account and what happens to your data when you do.

1. **Deleting Your Account from the App** — Open the application, go to **Profile**, and tap **Delete Account**. A confirmation dialog will ask you to confirm the request; once confirmed, the deletion is applied immediately and cannot be undone.
2. **Requesting Deletion by Email** — If you no longer have access to the application, you can request the deletion of your account by writing to **reelrivals877@gmail.com** from any email address, indicating the **username** of the account concerned. This information is required to identify the account, as accounts are identified by username rather than by email address. Requests received by email are processed within **7 days**.
3. **What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted** — The account is anonymised: the username is replaced by an anonymous identifier of the form `deleted-xxxxx`, and the associated credentials are invalidated, which makes signing in to the account permanently impossible. The account cannot be restored or reactivated afterwards.
4. **Content You Have Already Posted** — Content published before the deletion (video links, titles, descriptions, ratings, comments and chat messages) remains visible inside the groups you belonged to, attributed to the anonymous `deleted-xxxxx` name. No cascade deletion is performed. This is intentional: Reel Rivals is built around closed groups, and removing a member's past messages would break the continuity of conversations and of the shared history for the other members of those groups. After deletion, this content is no longer linked to an identifiable account.
5. **Data Deleted and Data Retained** — Deleted: your username and your profile picture, together with the ability to authenticate as this account. Retained: the content described in section 4, under the anonymous identifier, for as long as the groups concerned exist. Data is hosted by Supabase Inc., which acts as a technical data processor.
6. **Other Requests Regarding Your Data** — For any other request relating to your data (access, rectification, portability, or a question about this page), contact **reelrivals877@gmail.com**.
