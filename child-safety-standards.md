# Child Safety Standards

Reel Rivals maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This page describes that commitment, what is prohibited, the safety measures already built into the application, and how to report a concern.

1. **Zero-Tolerance Commitment** — Reel Rivals does not permit, host, promote, or facilitate child sexual abuse material (CSAM), the sexualization of minors in any form, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or any other form of child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE) — whether in shared video links, titles, descriptions, comments, ratings, chat messages, profile pictures, or crew names and crew photos. This applies to every piece of content and every interaction the application allows, with no exception.
2. **Scope** — This policy applies to all users, to everything that can be posted or shared inside the application (video links, titles, captions, ratings, comments, chat messages), and to all profile and crew imagery. It applies regardless of whether the content originates on Reel Rivals or is a link to a third-party platform (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook, etc.) — sharing a link to CSAE content hosted elsewhere is treated exactly the same as posting it directly.
3. **Age Requirement** — Reel Rivals is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. By creating an account, a user represents that they meet this minimum age. The application does not knowingly collect personal data from a child under 13; if we become aware that an account belongs to a child under this age, that account and its data are removed. See our Privacy Policy for how account data is handled.
4. **Closed, Invite-Only Crews** — Reel Rivals is not an open social network. Every "crew" (group) is private and can only be joined through an invitation code shared by an existing member. There is no public feed, no public search, and no way to discover users or groups from outside a crew someone has already been invited into. This structural design significantly limits the ability of a stranger to reach or contact a minor through the application.
5. **In-App Reporting** — Every video clip shared inside a crew can be reported directly from the app (the "Report" action on the clip), which lets the reporting user describe the concern in their own words before it is sent for review. This is the fastest way to flag suspected CSAE content for removal, and it never requires leaving the app.
6. **Reporting What the In-App Tool Doesn't Cover** — The in-app report action currently covers shared video clips. For anything else — a chat message, a comment, a profile picture, a crew name or crew photo, a username, or any other conduct that may put a child at risk — email **reelrivals877@gmail.com** with as much detail as possible: the crew name, the username(s) involved, the approximate date/time, and a description of the content or behavior. Reports alleging CSAE or any other form of child endangerment are treated as the highest priority.
7. **Moderation and Review** — Each crew's admin can review reports filed within that crew and immediately remove the reported clip. Independently of crew-level moderation, the publisher (reachable at reelrivals877@gmail.com) personally reviews every report that alleges CSAE or another form of child endangerment — this is never left to crew moderation alone.
8. **Removing Content and Accounts** — Content confirmed or reasonably suspected to involve CSAE is removed as soon as it is identified, without waiting for a full investigation to conclude. A crew admin can ban or remove a member from their crew at any time, and any member can leave a crew themselves. On a confirmed violation, the publisher permanently disables the offending account. Creating a new account to circumvent a removal made for this reason is itself a violation of these Standards and of our Terms of Service.
9. **Preservation and Cooperation with Authorities** — Where legally required or appropriate, information about a reported account and the content involved is preserved, and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the competent law enforcement authorities. The publisher cooperates fully with any resulting investigation and does not notify a suspected offender before or during that process.
10. **No Retaliation** — A user who reports suspected CSAE or any other child-safety concern in good faith will never be penalized, banned, or otherwise disadvantaged for making that report, even if the report is ultimately found to be unsubstantiated.
11. **Designated Point of Contact** — Kélyan EDMOND, publisher of Reel Rivals, is the designated point of contact for child safety matters relating to this application. Contact: **reelrivals877@gmail.com**.
12. **Policy Updates** — This policy may be updated as the application evolves. The version in effect at any given time is the one published at this address.
